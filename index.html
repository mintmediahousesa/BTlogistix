The vehicle for your job

BT owns a Ford Bantam for suitable local loads. A Hyundai H100 is hired per job when a larger vehicle is needed.

Vehicle choice and the accepted load are confirmed after BT checks the items, dimensions and estimated weight. H100 hire is subject to availability and included in the agreed quotation where required.

Loading, assembly & protection

  • Loading help is available. Mention stairs, lifts, parking and carrying distances at both locations.
  • Furniture assembly is available by arrangement. Include the items and assembly needed in your request so it can be quoted.
  • Protective wrapping and furniture blankets are not supplied. Please arrange suitable protection before collection and discuss fragile items with BT.
